A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Questions.
Answered.

How do I install it? +

Find DealHound in the ChatGPT apps directory, connect it, and tell it your list — brands, categories, keywords. That’s the whole setup.

Does it watch when I’m not chatting? +

Yes. Scheduled tasks and MCP Events keep your watchlist live 24/7, so matches surface even when ChatGPT is closed. That’s the whole point of a dots-native watcher.

Where do the deals come from? +

Reddit deal communities, Slickdeals, Ben’s Bargains, and Hip2Save. Before we alert you, the retailer’s current price and stock are checked — so you see what it actually costs now, not what a post said it cost.

Is my watchlist private? +

Your watchlist is stored with your ChatGPT connection. We don’t sell your data, and we don’t use your list for anything but finding your deals.

Do links cost me extra? +

No. When you buy through a link we share, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. The price you pay is the price the retailer shows.

Can a deal sell out before I see it? +

Yes. Prices change and deals sell out — hot ones especially. Always check the retailer’s current price and availability before buying.
