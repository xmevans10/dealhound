Fair questions.

Here’s the
straight answer.

Good deals deserve clear terms. So does the tool that finds them.

Is this an Amazon price tracker?

No. DealHound matches your brands, categories, and keywords against posts from Reddit deal communities, Slickdeals, Ben’s Bargains, and Hip2Save. It isn’t a price-history tracker for a single Amazon product.

How do alerts reach me?

By email. Free sends a daily digest. Pro sends instant alerts as matching deals fire. Each alert includes the price, discount, why it matched, and the deal link. The same deal won’t be sent twice in 24 hours.

What does Pro unlock?

For $4.99 once, Pro unlocks unlimited watchlist terms and instant alerts. It also includes RestockRadar back-in-stock alerts when that feature ships; it is not available yet. Your license key arrives by email after checkout.

Do I need an account?

No. Free needs no account, key, or signup. Email delivery needs a destination email address. Pro uses a cryptographically signed license key verified offline, with no account, login, or phone-home license check.

How is the free plan free?

Affiliate links help fund it. Some deal links earn DealHound a commission if you make a purchase, at no extra cost to you. Free remains $0 forever.

What if a deal sells out?

It happens. Deals expire, stock runs out, and third-party prices can change. An alert is a heads-up, not a guarantee. Always check the retailer’s current price and availability before buying.
