Where do the deals come from?
Your brief suggests Reddit deal communities, Slickdeals, Ben’s Bargains, and Hip2Save. Your agent uses the sources its tools can access, then checks the retailer’s current price and stock.
How do I connect my agent?
Build your watchlist, choose your assistant, then copy or download your shopping brief. Paste it into Muse, ChatGPT, or another agent. This is a manual handoff; native account connections aren’t available on this page.
Can you guarantee a deal will still be there?
No. Prices change and deals sell out. Check the retailer’s current price and availability before buying.
Can my agent monitor prices for me?
That depends on your agent’s tools and access. The brief asks it to explain what it can do and confirm how ongoing checks would work. Copying the brief doesn’t automatically activate monitoring.
