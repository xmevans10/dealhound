A FEW THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Your rules.
How is DealHound free?
There are no DealHound subscriptions or search fees. We may earn affiliate commissions on eligible purchases through our links, at no extra cost to you. Buying anything is optional. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.
Do I have to make a watchlist here?
No. Start in your agent. It can ask what you want or suggest interests from context you have shared for this task. You confirm inferred interests before searching. No DealHound account is required.
Can it see everything my agent knows?
No. DealHound cannot read your private chat history, emails or other connected accounts. It receives only the shopping terms and filters your agent sends to its tools.
Where do the deals come from?
Tell your agent what you need and your budget. It uses its available search tools to look for relevant products and potential savings. DealHound turns suitable Amazon.com finds into convenient, disclosed shopping links. Check the retailer for current prices, sizes and shipping; savings and stock are not guaranteed.
Will it check prices while I’m away?
This release is on demand: ask your agent to search. DealHound does not save a shopping profile or start background monitoring.
Is the connector available now?
Public installation is pending platform review. The starter prompt is a conversation preview, not an installation. Muse support is planned.
