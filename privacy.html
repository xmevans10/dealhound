Privacy

Last updated October 1, 2026. DealHound is preparing for public release. This policy describes the website and the intended hosted connector.

What we receive

The connector receives only the shopping interests and filters your agent sends for each search. There is no DealHound account or login. We do not receive full chat history, email inboxes, other connected accounts, payment information or passwords.

Use and retention

Tool inputs are processed to answer the current search. The application does not persist shopping interests, preferences or user identifiers and does not log tool inputs. We do not sell shopping interests or use them for advertising. We cache source feeds for five minutes; those caches contain public listings, not user queries. A shared rate counter limits requests without individual identifiers.

Providers and control

Cloudflare hosts the service and may process network and security metadata under its own policies. Deal sources receive server-side requests for their configured feed, without your shopping queries. Your agent provider handles conversations and any memory under its own policies. Disconnect DealHound in your agent to stop future calls. Conversation history is controlled through your agent provider.

This website

The current website uses no analytics cookies and does not collect a shopping profile. Copying a starter prompt only writes to your clipboard when you click the button. Google Fonts requests expose network metadata to Google. Product images are served from this site. If you used an earlier version, it may have stored a list in this browser; use the button below to remove that local data.

Questions and requests

Use our support page for privacy questions or deletion assistance. Do not include passwords, access tokens, private shopping data or identifying documents in public issues.
