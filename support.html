DealHound

Connection help

DealHound starts in your agent, not with a form on this website.

Public installation

The public ChatGPT listing is not available yet. When published, the Add DealHound button will open the real directory listing. Install it and ask what to look for. No DealHound account or login is required. Muse support is planned; no Muse installation is currently available.

After connecting

Try: “Help me find a quiet mechanical keyboard under $100.” Your agent asks for any missing country or currency, supplies interests and filters with each search. It can also suggest interests from context you’ve shared for this task. DealHound cannot inspect private conversation history or other linked accounts.

Your data

DealHound does not store a shopping profile. Your agent controls its conversation history and memory; use its settings to manage them. Disconnect DealHound to stop future calls.

Developer connection

A stable hosted HTTPS /mcp endpoint is required; choose No Authentication. This repository includes the Cloudflare deployment setup. A local preview and the starter prompt do not install a connector. See deployment instructions.

Contact

Open a support issue. Include the tool name, time and non-sensitive error message. Never post passwords, bearer tokens or private account data. For private support, email xmevans10@gmail.com. DealHound is published by Xander E.
